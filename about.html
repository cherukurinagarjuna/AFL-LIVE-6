My point of view

Consistency is a skill, not a personality trait.

Most people don’t need another extreme plan. They need a clear place to begin, a way to make the plan fit their real week, and support when the week inevitably gets messy.

That is what coaching is for. We work with your food preferences, schedule, energy, and goals—then turn them into simple actions you can repeat.
